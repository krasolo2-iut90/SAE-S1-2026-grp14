An Integrated Business Model

OVHcloud mainly sells subscriptions and cloud services billed monthly or based on consumption. The company designs, assembles, and operates a large portion of its own servers and data centers, rather than renting all of its infrastructure from third parties. This integration reduces certain hosting costs and allows for better control over performance, security, and data localization.

Its revenue comes from three main areas: Private Cloud (dedicated servers and reserved infrastructure), Public Cloud (on-demand computing resources), and Webcloud (website hosting, domain names, and associated services). The model is based on recurring revenue, supplemented by services and options tailored to customer needs.
